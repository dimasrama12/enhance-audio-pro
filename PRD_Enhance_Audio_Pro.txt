PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
======================================================================
Project Name: Enhance Audio Pro
Suggested Alternative Names: VoxEnhance Studio, ClarityPro Audio, SonicRefine, PureVoice Desktop
Target Platforms: Windows (.exe) and macOS (.dmg / .app)
======================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Objective: A professional desktop application designed to assist audio and video editors in enhancing, separating, and manipulating audio tracks effortlessly.
* Internet Requirement (Suggestion): It is highly recommended to build this as an OFFLINE application (Local Processing). This guarantees privacy, zero upload/download limits, and avoids expensive cloud server costs. AI models will be downloaded once during installation or first launch.
* Recommended Tech Stack: 
  - Frontend UI: Tauri (Rust-based) or Electron.js. Tauri is highly recommended for a smaller app size and better performance. Framework: React.js or Svelte with Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion for smooth animations.
  - Audio Processing Backend: Python (compiled to a background executable via PyInstaller) utilizing libraries like `torchaudio`, `librosa`, `ffmpeg`.
  - AI Models: `Demucs` or `Spleeter` (for instrument/stem separation) and `DeepFilterNet` or custom models (for noise/echo removal).

2. CORE FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* AI Speech Enhancement: Remove background noise and echo from voice recordings. Fixes audio problems post-recording, eliminating the need for a perfect microphone.
* Advanced Stem/Source Separation:
  - Isolate specific elements: Vocals, Noise, Background Music.
  - Isolate specific instruments: Drums, Electric Guitar, Bass, Vocals, etc.
* Multi-Format Media Support: 
  - Import video and audio files (over 300+ formats supported, automatically converted internally for faster operation).
* Input Methods: Drag-and-drop file support, standard file browser, and direct in-app voice recording.
* Processing Limits: No maximum file size or duration limit. Batch processing capacity: upload up to 30 audio files and 10 video files simultaneously.

3. EDITING & MANIPULATION TOOLS (AUDIO TAB)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Audio Trimming & Cutting: Adjust start and end times visually.
* Speed & Pitch Adjustment: Sliders to change playback speed (with defined min/max limits) and shift pitch smoothly.
* Volume & Decibel (dB) Boost: Safely amplify audio volume.
* Auto Fade In/Out: One-click buttons to add fade effects, with customizable fade duration (in seconds).
* Audio Merging & Crossfade: 
  - Visual drag-and-drop track timeline to combine unlimited tracks seamlessly. 
  - Crossfade support for smooth transitions between clips.
  - Adjustable playback intervals using blue sliders (controllable via mouse or arrow keys).
* Audio Looping: Custom loop generator (e.g., extend a 1-minute clip automatically up to 2 hours).
* Playback Controls: Real-time Play and Stop buttons for previewing edits.
* Reset Functionality: Individual 'Reset' buttons for each specific effect (volume, pitch, etc.), plus a global 'Reset All Editing' button.

4. ADVANCED EQUALIZER (EQ)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Frequency Sliders: Adjustable bands from 0% to 100% on 0, 60, 170, 310, 600, 1k, 3k, 6k, 12k, 14k, 16k Hz.
* Presets: Default, Classic, Dance, Club, Full Bass, Full Bass & Treble, Full Treble, Laptop Speakers, Large Hall, Live, Party, Pop, Reggae, Rock, Ska, Soft, Soft Rock, Techno Rock.

5. PREVIEW & COMPARISON CAPABILITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Real-time Video/Audio Preview: Apply enhanced audio directly to the uploaded video timeline for instant visual and audio playback.
* A/B Testing Switch: Toggle between 'Original Audio' and 'Enhanced Audio' seamlessly to compare results.
* Dedicated Preview Tabs: Separate views to listen to just the newly enhanced audio versus the old audio.
* Strength Adjustment: Slider to control the intensity of the AI enhancement, balancing speech, music, and ambient noise for the most natural sound.

6. BATCH PROCESSING & QUEUE MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Data Grid Columns: No | Filename | Destination | Size | From | Output Status.
* Selection & Deletion: Multi-select support using Ctrl+Click (individual) and Shift+Click (range). Ability to clear items from the queue.
* Organization & Filters: 
  - Group files by audio/video format.
  - Filter by Output Status (e.g., Incomplete, Processing, Done).
  - Search bar to quickly find specific files in the queue.
* Status Indicators: Display the total number of files in the queue and show full history (including incomplete processing).
* Output Flexibility: Choose target output format manually per file, or apply a format globally to the entire batch.

7. UI/UX & VISUAL DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Aesthetic: Professional, high-end, compact, but well-structured.
* Animations: Smooth micro-animations on hover for interactive elements to make the app feel alive.
* View Modes: Toggle switch between Grid View and List View.
* Tabbed Layout: Clear separation between the Video Upload Tab and Audio Upload Tab.

8. SETTINGS & CUSTOMIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Themes: Light and Dark mode toggle.
* Languages: Multilingual support (English, Chinese, Spanish, Indonesian, German, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Arabic, Turkish, Thai, Vietnamese, Russian, Italian, Korean, Polish, Dutch).

10. SUGGESTIONS (FEATURES YOU MIGHT HAVE MISSED)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Hardware Acceleration (GPU): AI audio processing is heavy on the CPU. Adding a feature to utilize the user's graphics card (NVIDIA CUDA / AMD) will drastically speed up rendering times.
* Waveform/Spectrogram Visualization: Visualizing the audio waves makes it much easier for users to trim audio accurately and visually identify noise.
* Auto-Save Projects: If the app is closed accidentally, an auto-save mechanism ensures the user's timeline and queue are not lost.
* Export Bitrate/Quality Options: In addition to choosing the format (MP3, WAV), users usually need to select the audio bitrate (e.g., 128kbps, 320kbps) and sample rate (44.1kHz, 48kHz) for professional use.
